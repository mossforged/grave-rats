# A014-01 — three ship pitches

Thumbnail order is left to right in A014-01.png and the review sheet. All names below describe purposes, not approved vessel names.

1. **Long-haul crew transport — the voyage that never ended.** A cramped passenger workforce ship whose life support failed compartment by compartment. Warm mess lighting and tightly repeated bunks make its emptiness personal. The bridge black box holds the oxygen rationing and diversion record needed to settle the loss. Room story: meals remain laid out in the mess while bedding has been moved into the last heated corridor; quarters farther from engineering were abandoned first.
2. **Biological survey vessel — containment outlived the crew. Recommended.** A small research crew carried live deep-range samples; the isolated laboratory still has power while human spaces have gone cold. The bridge black box stores quarantine orders, navigation and mirrored specimen telemetry, making it the recovery prize rather than another glowing sample. Room story: a meal trolley stopped at the laboratory seal, a folding screen was pushed aside, and a sample case reached the emergency bridge. The crew tried to preserve routine, then containment, then command. No bodies or exposition panels are needed.
3. **Mining tug — the machine got the space.** A working tug whose generator and tow-control cabinets have displaced almost everything intended for people. Cyan engineering dominates; small amber bunks and mess tables are squeezed against its machinery. The bridge black box holds tow-load and reactor-trip records for an accident claim. Room story: stripped spare busbars on the mess table and bedding beside the warm generator show the crew cannibalising their living space to keep the tow alive.

Pick 2 because engineering, bio lab, bridge, quarters and mess each serve the vessel's purpose without repeating the hospital/cargo ship. Cyan-green specimens, emergency-red command, cold bunks and warm abandoned meals give strong navigation landmarks using the existing room kit. The recommended plan and A014-03 through A014-05 develop this pitch only. The other two thumbnail compositions are mood alternatives, not additional layouts to build.
