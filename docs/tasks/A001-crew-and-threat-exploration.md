# A001 - Crew and threat concept exploration (art)

**Read:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/handoffs/game-brief.md`, `docs/concepts/C001-dead-circuit-key-art/concept-sheet.jpg`. **Needs:** nothing.
**Autonomy:** human-gated (John picks directions; nothing here goes in the game directly).

Grave Rats needs a face. The crew are broke salvagers in bulky pressure suits, two hundred years into an even worse capitalism, who board derelicts overrun by rogue AI, robots, aliens and horrors, and fight rival pirate crews, all for credits. The tone is satirical and desperate; the combat is weighty and visceral. This is an exploration pass: wide on purpose, so John can point at what he likes.

## Do
Generate with ImageGen and save each image as `assets/source/A001-crew-threat/A001-NN.png` with `A001-NN.prompt.md` beside it.

1. **Crew silhouettes (A001-01 … 06).** Six salvager suit designs on a neutral dark background, full body, front three-quarter view, the same scale in each image.
   - All must read as **grubby blue-collar space workers**, not soldiers or heroes: patched suits, mismatched plates, tool belts, salvage tags, cheap helmets. Add satirical corporate wear: invented sponsor patches, debt-ID stencils, "property of" stamps, warning labels. No real brands.
   - Vary the silhouettes: bulky hauler, lean cutter, heavy rig with a big pack, medic-scrounger, tech with a drone pack, a wildcard.
   - Each has one strong colour accent so four crew read apart from above (orange, yellow, teal, red as a starting palette).
2. **Same crew from the game camera (A001-07, 08).** The six designs shown from a steep overhead three-quarter view, small (like the concept's operators), to test readability from above.
3. **Threat directions (A001-09 … 14).** One image each, gritty and grounded, menacing rather than gory, each shown in a ship interior in the concept's lighting:
   - a rogue ship AI and what it does to a ship (hijacked systems, drones, turrets, the ship itself hunting you);
   - security robots gone wrong;
   - an acid-spitting alien stalker (John's example: it stalks into a medbay while the crew hides behind an overturned table);
   - a horror: something that wears the dead crew's suits, or worse;
   - a rival pirate crew: the satirical opposite of ours (better funded, worse people);
   - a wildcard.
4. **Key art variant (A001-15).** The concept's medbay–corridor–cargo view with the crew from (1) instead of the generic operators, same camera and mood.
5. **Contact sheets** in `docs/screens/A001/`: `crew.png` (01–08, labelled) and `threats.png` (09–15, labelled).

## Acceptance
- 15 images, each with its prompt file, plus two labelled contact sheets.
- `docs/handoffs/auto-A001.md`: a line per image on the idea behind it, anything refused or skipped, and your own top pick for crew and threat with one sentence why.
- **Human (John):** picks which crew looks and threat directions to develop.

Out of scope: final character sheets, turnarounds, 3D. Those come after John picks.
