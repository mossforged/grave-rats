# Playtest log (John's words, dated, newest first)

## 2026-10-30 — stage 2 grey box (`stalker_deck`, after T005), John's first play
- Tense and fun? "The tension is going to come down to how dangerous these things are, how fast they move, how much damage they do. I wouldn't say that I'm particularly scared at this point." "It attacks pretty slowly. It doesn't really move."
- Sight: "having your vision only be in the circle around you and the flashlight, I think that that helps."
- Flashlight: "I'm a little bit confused about the two modes of the flashlight. The first one seems more atmospheric, but it's also kind of like flickering a bunch in a weird way. The second one is less atmospheric and it only goes out to a certain distance." (lead: F is light on/off; "off" leaves only the awareness circle. The flicker is unexplained.)
- Ambush: "I heard it. I don't feel too late. It feels very easy. ... in the multiple times I've done it, I've been hit by the monster once."
- Doors: stalkers shouldn't open them; "I think they should try to break through. I don't think it should be fast."
- Tipping a bed: "in theory it could be" worth it; "it's a cool idea". Cover needs "a monster that shoots something", and "maybe having a sort of Gears style, like, lock into cover thing would be a good idea, where you, like, brace against it and then can shoot over it."
- Bug: "if I'm just standing and I press C to crouch, his torso sort of, like, corkscrews around in a weird way."
- Shield: "I don't see a green shield on a HUD. I don't see any indication that it is shielding me. I see crouched." (lead: the mark only draws while a stalker is hunting you.)
- Stalker: "too easy at this point. It's not really dodging. It's not fast. Its shot is kind of slow."
- Sounds: audible.
- Weight: "Turning, aiming, reloading. Those seem pretty good right now." Cursor above the cone: "I don't think it's confusing yet." Aim cone readable; "I don't know that in the final game we necessarily have to have a visible aim cone."
- Darkness: "So far, so good." Unexplored should be dark. Awareness circle: "It might be a little bit big." Tall cargo crates should block sight: "yeah, probably." Didn't notice the shield warning.
- Camera: "Probably one. I think that one might be a little bit zoomed out, but two feels too close. ... somewhere between 1 and 2, a little bit closer to 1 than 2." Room and character scale keys "don't do anything" (lead: he was in `stalker_deck`; those keys only work in `scale_deck`).
- Scale deck: cut-away "I definitely prefer the sort of quasi see-through cutaway rather than the like the dropping out down the wall cutaway. I think it could even be a little bit more obscure." Scale: "I like the look of the bigger one, the player model ... I think he is generally, I think, too small ... We want to be able to see details in the model"; rooms: "I think I like tight and bigger for the scale."
- Art: crew "the orange one I think is probably fine ... having variation in their look and size eventually would be good." Environment sheets: "this all looks very cool." HUD: "I like A2 the best" (suit OS); liked the ring around the operator in 01. Briefing/screens: "all of that's cool." Logo: "I like one. I like the cracked visor. ... I like the crew patch too." Title art: "Probably 8 ... him doing the door is cool. Maybe the first guy doing the door and the other sort of walking up behind him."
