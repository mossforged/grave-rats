## One-line briefs

- **A016-01 Plasma cutter:** Door cutting / close denial; held beam, heat-limited; ammo: replaceable power cell; flash/sound/impact: cyan slit flare; electrical rasp; molten scar; hook: Cutting time billed by the second.
- **A016-02 Rivet driver:** Single-target stagger / pin; slow single shots; ammo: captive rivet cassette; flash/sound/impact: brief orange jet; piston slam; hard metal punch; hook: Fasteners remain company property.
- **A016-03 Nail gun:** Cheap close suppression; fast bursts; ammo: collated nail cassette; flash/sound/impact: tiny amber puff; rattling chatter; small sharp chips; hook: Collation surcharge per strip.
- **A016-04 Flare gun:** Light placement / route marking; slow single shots; ammo: detachable flare magazine; flash/sound/impact: red launch bloom; hollow pop and hiss; burning light pool; hook: Illumination is a premium service.
- **A016-05 Breach ram:** Door breach / pushback; slow committed bash; ammo: sealed pressure cartridge; flash/sound/impact: no flash; hydraulic cough; deep impact clang; hook: Door damage waiver excludes doors.
- **A016-06 Tether harpoon:** Anchor / restrain / retrieval; one launch then reel; ammo: reusable tether bolt + cable spool; flash/sound/impact: amber gas puff; cable whine; anchoring thunk; hook: Cable loss deducted from next shift.
- **A016-07 Issue rifle:** General-purpose baseline; controlled automatic; ammo: company box magazine; flash/sound/impact: short warm flash; dry action clack + punch + room slap; surface-specific chips; hook: Rental continues while unloaded.
- **A016-08 Budget shotgun:** Close doorway burst; slow self-loading; ammo: detachable shell magazine; flash/sound/impact: broad brief warm fan; bass crack and action clunk; clustered impacts; hook: Every pellet separately accounted.
- **A016-09 Subscription smart-pistol:** Convenient aim / ammo dependency; semi-auto; ammo: signed paid magazine; flash/sound/impact: small warm star; chirp + dry crack; precise chip; hook: Authorization expires with magazine.
- **A016-10 Repossession launcher:** Displace targets / break cover; one shot then pump; ammo: impact-only foam-baton cartridge magazine; flash/sound/impact: small amber gas cloud; hollow thump + pump clack; shove and dust; hook: Repossession fee charged to possessor.
- **A016-11 Debt-counter sidearm:** Carry-with-salvage backup; semi-auto; ammo: standard pistol magazine; flash/sound/impact: brief warm flash; cheap metallic crack; modest impact; hook: Counter rises with each shot.
- **A016-12 Prior Claim scoped rifle:** Long lane precision / tell; slow semi-auto; ammo: precision box magazine; flash/sound/impact: tight warm flash; sharp crack with long room tail; focused metal spark; hook: Precision financed by Lien Capital.
- **A016-13 Prior Claim shield + SMG:** Protected advance / flank tradeoff; short automatic bursts; ammo: compact box magazine; flash/sound/impact: small rapid warm flashes; clipped chatter; clustered chips; hook: Protection excludes deductible.
- **A016-14 Prior Claim autocannon:** Committed sustained lane control; slow automatic; ammo: oversized box magazine; flash/sound/impact: large brief warm flash; mechanical hammer + low punch; heavy debris; hook: Carry assistance sold separately.
- **A016-15 Frag charge:** Flush entrenched targets; one throw / fuse; ammo: single-use charge; flash/sound/impact: brief amber burst; sharp crack + room boom; debris/scorch; hook: Disposal fee applies after detonation.
- **A016-16 Flashbang:** Brief visibility / interrupt; single throw; ammo: single-use flash canister; flash/sound/impact: brief white pulse; hard snap + ringing; no damage crater; hook: Vision recovery outside coverage.
- **A016-17 Motion mine:** Route alarm / area denial; single deploy / delayed arm; ammo: single-use sensor puck; flash/sound/impact: armed red blink then amber burst; spaced tick + blast; localized sparks; hook: Unauthorized motion incurs penalty.
