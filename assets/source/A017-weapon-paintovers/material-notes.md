# A017 — palette and material placement

These are paintover instructions for the lead's existing model slots, not changes to game materials. The blockout remains the geometry authority. Length annotations come from the lead's review sheet: rifle 0.94 m, shotgun 0.84 m, flare gun 0.27 m, cutter 0.67 m, rivet driver 0.83 m, frag 0.16 m, beacon 0.24 m. These supersede the older A016 concept dimensions for this packet; they are not manufacturing drawings.

## Shared finish

- `hull`: desaturated industrial teal; large matte painted planes, scratches confined primarily to edges.
- `hazard`: worn ochre yellow; identifying panels and small end caps. Keep bright areas broad enough to read from above.
- `clinical`: dirty ivory, not pure white; medium matte finish and restrained oil streaks.
- `steel`: dark cool exposed metal, brushed on wear faces; avoid mirror highlights that compete with the silhouette.
- `rubber`: near-black grips, pads and bumpers; matte, lighter scuffing on corners.
- Emissives: very small status/lens areas only. No active firing effects. Bloom is not proof of gameplay lighting.
- Grilles, rivets, micro labels and panel seams are proposed surface/normal detail. They must not drive new protruding geometry or obscure the simple part layout.

## A017-01 — Issue rifle

hull teal stock and receiver; clinical ivory vented front shroud; hazard yellow magazine foot and small top tab; steel barrel and rail; rubber grip and butt pad. No emissive except a tiny dim red sight status dot.

Readability anchor: teal rear mass versus ivory nose, with a narrow yellow magazine foot.

## A017-02 — Budget shotgun

clinical ivory stock and receiver, hazard yellow thin roof plate and magazine foot; steel twin longitudinal tubes; rubber foregrip, pistol grip and butt pad. No emissives.

Readability anchor: ivory long body and yellow roof, dark twin front tubes.

## A017-03 — Flare gun / flare pistol

hazard yellow body and large barrel; steel muzzle collar and magazine shoe; rubber black grip and rear block; tiny dull red lens confined to muzzle face, no emitted flare.

Readability anchor: yellow barrel/body over a single dark grip.

## A017-04 — Plasma cutter

hazard yellow central casing; steel six cooling ribs, top tank, stock and fork; rubber twin grips and butt pad; cyan emitter lens only, unpowered with no beam.

Readability anchor: yellow ribbed body, dark open fork and small cyan lens.

## A017-05 — Rivet driver

hazard yellow stock, cylinder, lower cassette and front block; steel carry handle, collar and lower receiver; rubber grip and butt pad. Tiny amber status inset on casing only.

Readability anchor: yellow cylinder and stock, dark elevated carry handle.

## A017-06 — Frag charge / thrown explosive charge

hull teal cylindrical shell; steel three dark raised bands and left end assembly; hazard yellow right end cap; muted brass existing pin tab; no emissives.

Readability anchor: teal cylinder broken by exactly three dark bands and a yellow cap.

## A017-07 — Decoy beacon

hull teal casing, rubber black angular end bumpers, hazard yellow existing rectangular loop handle, steel flush speaker grille and fasteners; tiny amber end status only.

Readability anchor: teal body between black bumpers and a large yellow loop.
