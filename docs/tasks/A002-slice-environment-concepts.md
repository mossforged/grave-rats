# A002 - Slice environment concepts: kit, props, signage (art)

**Read:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/handoffs/game-brief.md`, `docs/design/tone-bible.md`, `docs/concepts/C001-dead-circuit-key-art/gameplay-panel.jpg`, `docs/screens/A001/sheet.png` (A001-08 and A001-15 for lighting). **Needs:** nothing.
**Autonomy:** human-gated (John picks; these guide stage 3's kit and the 3D pieces).

Stage 3 builds the medbay–corridor–cargo slice at concept quality. Before anyone models a wall, we need clear, consistent design sheets for the pieces the slice is made of, drawn the way a level artist would brief a modeller. This also gives the satire somewhere to live: signage, labels and warnings aboard the ship.

## Do
Save each image as `assets/source/A002-slice-env/A002-NN.png` with `A002-NN.prompt.md` beside it.

1. **Wall module sheet (A002-01, 02).** Orthographic-style design sheets on a neutral background, front and three-quarter views.
   - **01, corridor and cargo walls:** a plain bay, a bay with a pipe run, a vent bay, a bay with a light strip, a wall screen, and a corner pilaster.
   - **02, medbay walls:** clinical panels, a supply niche, a red-cross plaque, a light strip.
   - Industrial, chunky, readable from a steep camera: thick tops and strong bevels. Show a human silhouette for scale (1.8 m).
2. **Doors and thresholds (A002-03).** A sliding bulkhead door (closed, half open, open), a locked door with an override panel, a breached door (something came through it), and a floor hatch.
3. **Floors (A002-04).** Deck plates, grate runs, hazard edging, drains, cable trays, and a wet or acid-damaged patch, shown as a top-down tile sheet.
4. **Medbay props (A002-05).** Treatment bed (upright and **overturned as cover**), surgical lamp, med cabinet, IV stand, gurney, a med console, and scattered supplies. Show the overturned bed from the game's steep overhead angle as well.
5. **Cargo props (A002-06).** Stacked crates in three sizes, a yellow ammo crate, a pallet, a shelving rack, a canister, a loader exoskeleton (parked, empty), and a cargo lift platform.
6. **Cover read (A002-07).** The slice's props from the game camera (steep overhead three-quarter, small scale), marked by **low cover** (crouch behind) vs **tall cover** (blocks sight), to test whether cover height is readable at a glance.
7. **Signage and decals (A002-08).** One sheet of ship signage in the tone bible's deadpan corporate voice, invented and short:
   - **room plates:** MEDBAY, CARGO 02, JUNCTION 01;
   - **warnings:** e.g. "AIRLOCK CYCLING FEE APPLIES", "SAFETY IS EVERYONE'S RESPONSIBILITY (EXCEPT MANAGEMENT'S)";
   - **hazard pictograms;**
   - **floor stencils;**
   - **a corporate logo** for the invented liquidator, *Remnant Asset Recovery*.
   Flat, clean graphics a modeller can use as decals.
8. **Lighting mood board (A002-09).** The same corridor bay lit four ways: clinical cool white (medbay spill), sodium amber (cargo), emergency red, and flashlight only in dark.
9. **Contact sheet** `docs/screens/A002/sheet.png`, labelled.
