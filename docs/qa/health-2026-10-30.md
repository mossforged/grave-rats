   The check records both: fixed step is repeatable (identical in every run), and real time matches the history but moves up to about 11% between runs.
2. **Low: the T006 and T007 bot tools aren't really seeded.** Repro: run `tools/t007_bots.gd` twice and diff. Expected: identical, as T007's "same seeds" says. Happened: the bot crouching behind cover and firing died in 7 of 12 runs, then 10 of 12. `_bot` calls `squad.reset()` after seeding, which builds new brains with random seeds, and neither tool seeds the player's rifle. In T006 only the bots that fire are affected. Earlier handoffs' small bot differences may be dice. `tools/qa/t006_seeded.gd` and `t007_seeded.gd` subclass the originals to fix this, and they were identical across two runs, including under parallel load.
3. **Low: engine error noise.** The tests pass while logging 414 `p_size <= 0` errors from `suit_os_hud.gd` `_text`. They appear only in headless logs, with none in the windowed captures, so players shouldn't see them. `tools/t008_flicker.gd` logs 324 `body->get_space() is null`; I didn't trace it, but it doesn't appear in the normal captures, so I suspect the tool disabling the stalkers. Counts are in the table at the bottom.
