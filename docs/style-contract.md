# Style contract (draft; fills in as stages 1 and 3 settle it)

- **Camera:** steep oblique perspective, about 55–65° pitch (set in T002, then fixed here).
- **Scale:** human 1.8 m; wall, door and corridor dimensions come out of T002.
- **Lighting:** dark ambient; practical pools by room type (medbay cool white, cargo sodium amber, engineering cyan); flashlight cones with dust; emissive trims and screens bloom, lit surfaces don't.
- **Materials:** worn industrial PBR; grime collects low and in corners; paint chips on edges.
- **Readability:** player, enemies, pickups and doorways must read at gameplay zoom first; beauty second.
- **Off-limits:** real brands and logos; other games' characters or ships.
