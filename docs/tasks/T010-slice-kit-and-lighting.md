# T010 - The slice kit, materials and lighting: grey box becomes ship (code)

**Modules:** `src/study/` (deck builder), new `src/kit/`, `tools/blender/` or an in-engine mesh builder (your choice; say why in the handoff), the lighting in the deck scenes. **Read:** `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/handoffs/auto-A006.md` (dimensions are the source of truth), `docs/screens/A006/sheet.png`, `docs/screens/A006/A006-kit-dimensions.png`, `assets/source/A006-slice/A006-05b.png`, `A006-06c.png`, `A006-07b.png`, `A006-08.png`, `assets/source/A002-slice-env/A002-08b.png` (signage), `A002-09b.png` (lighting), the `src/study/`, `src/doors/`, `src/sight/` and `src/camera/` contracts. **Needs:** T009 merged.
**Autonomy:** human-gated (stage 3: *"Does it match or beat the concept?"*).

John approved A006 as the stage 3 target: paint-overs of our real frames at concept quality, plus kit sheets drawn to the game's dimensions. Scripts build everything boxy and grid-snapped. John makes the irregular props in Meshy (hauler done; stalker, bed and console coming), so those stay as today's boxes until his files land.

## Do
1. **Kit pieces** at A006's dimensions, generated by script (Blender in `tools/blender/` exporting glb, or meshes built in Godot), and never hand-placed:
   - **walls:** corridor plain, pipe/vent, cargo reinforced, medbay clinical panel; ≤ 2 m bays plus the closing pieces the ×0.85 rooms need; corner and cap pieces;
   - **doors:** frame, jambs and header; a leaf with closed, open, locked (red light), dented mid-bash (driven by the T006 damage value) and broken-flat states;
   - **floors:** deck plate, grate runs, hazard edging, drains;
   - **cover props that are boxes:** low crate, tall crate stack, ammo crate.
   The deck builder places kit pieces where it now places boxes. Collision, sight occluders, cover classes and the cut-away fade keep working: the fade uses the kit's full-height walls (A006-05b).
2. **Materials** from A006-08: painted hull plate, grimy deck, grate, clinical panel, hazard trim, rubber, glass, emissive strips. Generate the textures procedurally (the legacy `tools/blender/textures.py` is a start) and keep grime low and in corners. All values in data.
3. **Lighting to the paint-overs:**
   - **practicals:** cool white in the medbay, sodium amber in cargo, emissive strips;
   - **atmosphere:** volumetric fog with dust in the flashlight cone;
   - **mood:** dark ambient, with the sight mask still the authority on what you know.
   Match `A006-01b`, `02b`, `03c` and `04b` from the same camera positions.
4. **Signage decals** from A002-08b: MEDBAY, CARGO 02, JUNCTION 01, one deadpan warning per room, floor stencils.
5. **Performance:** keep `pirate_deck` and `stalker_deck` under 8.3 ms average (120 fps) on the Mac. Report the cost of fog and lights separately.

## Acceptance
- **Headless:**
  - all old tests pass;
  - kit pieces match the A006 dimensions within 1 cm;
  - collision, occluders and cover classes are unchanged against T009 (the same tests);
  - every piece comes from the builder (none hand-placed);
  - all material and light values come from data.
- **Windowed** (`docs/screens/T010/`): the same four frames as the A006 paint-overs, side by side with them (a sheet like A006's), plus the cut-away fade, a door in each state, and frame time with fog on and off.
- **Human (John):** does it match or beat the concept?

Out of scope: Meshy props (bed, console, stalker) beyond leaving clean slots for them, new rooms, gameplay changes.
