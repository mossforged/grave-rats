# A004 - Logo and title art (art)

**Read:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/handoffs/game-brief.md`, `docs/design/tone-bible.md`, `docs/screens/A001/sheet.png`, `docs/screens/A003/sheet.png`. **Needs:** nothing.
**Autonomy:** human-gated (John picks; "Grave Rats" is the working title, so keep the logo easy to redo).

The game needs a face on its title screen and its store page one day. Explore the wordmark and one piece of title art, in the same grimy industrial language as the company screens. The logo is the *game's*, not Remnant Asset Recovery's: scrappier, less corporate, a bit of black humour.

## Do
Save as `assets/source/A004-title/A004-NN.png` with prompts beside each.
1. **Wordmark directions (A004-01 … 06).** Six "GRAVE RATS" wordmarks on dark backgrounds:
   - stencil spray-paint on a hull plate;
   - welded scrap letters;
   - a hazard-label style;
   - a cracked visor-HUD style;
   - a crude crew patch or embroidered badge (a rat in a pressure helmet, if it fits);
   - your wildcard.
   Must read at small sizes. Show each at large size and at 64 px tall in the same image.
2. **Crew patch / emblem (A004-07).** A crew-patch emblem usable as an app icon: the rat-in-a-helmet idea, or better.
3. **Title art (A004-08, 09).** The crew from A001 (01 orange hauler, 02 yellow cutter, 04 red medic, 03 teal rig) on a derelict's outer hull or airlock in the Drift, a graveyard of wrecks around them. Leave space for the logo. One wide 16:9 and one tall 2:3 (store capsule).
4. **Contact sheet** `docs/screens/A004/sheet.png`, labelled.

## Acceptance
- Nine images with prompts, plus the sheet.
- `docs/handoffs/auto-A004.md`: a line per image, which wordmarks survive at 64 px, and your pick.

Out of scope: final vector logo (that comes after John's pick), fonts licensing.
