# A010 - Company graphics: decals, posters and signage for the slice (art)

**Read:** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/design/tone-bible.md`, `docs/studies/2026-10-30-comparable-games.md` (the tone section: satire lives in fees, paperwork and the company's straight voice, not in quips; loud outside danger, terse inside it), `assets/source/A002-slice-env/A002-08b.png` (the signage language), `docs/screens/A003/sheet.png` (briefing, payslip, death bill), `docs/screens/A006/sheet.png` (the slice target). **Needs:** nothing.
**Autonomy:** human-gated (John approves the copy and look).

T010 builds the slice in the real kit and needs decals. The ship is a Remnant Asset Recovery (liquidator) job on a dead company's vessel, so two voices share the walls: the **dead ship's original owner** (faded, earnest, now ironic) and **Remnant's** fresh stickers and tape over the top. The satire lives here, on the walls and in the paperwork, deadpan and specific, never quipping.

## Do
Flat, clean, front-on graphics on a plain background (no wall texture, no perspective), so they can be cut out as decals. Save to `assets/source/A010-graphics/` as `A010-NN.png` with a `.prompt.md` each. Text must be legible and spelled right; note any garbling.
1. **The dead ship's owner (A010-01):** an invented line (shipping, mining or hospital-ship company) with its logo, a crew-morale poster, a safety poster and a "years without incident" board stopped at a grim number.
2. **Remnant overlays (A010-02):** inventory stickers, "PROPERTY OF REMNANT ASSET RECOVERY — DO NOT SALVAGE (UNLESS CONTRACTED)", hazard tape, "ASSESSED" stamps, liability notices, a QR-style "scan to accept terms" plate (no real QR codes).
3. **Room plates and wayfinding (A010-03):** MEDBAY, JUNCTION 01, CARGO 02, CREW QUARTERS, ENGINEERING; arrows; deck numbers; "AIRLOCK CYCLING FEE APPLIES".
4. **Floor stencils (A010-04):** hazard edging, "KEEP CLEAR", load limits, drain markings, a "SALVAGE STAGING AREA" box.
5. **Medbay set (A010-05):** triage chart, "co-pay required before resuscitation", an organ-donation-by-default notice, and the red-cross plaque.
6. **Contact sheet** `docs/screens/A010/sheet.png`.
Keep the copy short and specific. Every joke should be something a real, tired corporation might actually print. No real brands, no profanity, no gore.

## Acceptance
- Five sheets with prompts, plus the contact sheet.
- `docs/handoffs/auto-A010.md`: a line per sheet, all the copy as text (so it can become decals and data), any garbled text, and your three favourite lines.
- **Human (John):** approves the copy and look for T010.
