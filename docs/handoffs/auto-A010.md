## Three favourite lines

1. **RECOVERY DOES NOT INCLUDE RESCUE.** — precise institutional liability language with immediate stakes.
2. **SIGNAL LOSS DOES NOT VOID ACCEPTANCE.** — the company anticipates a practical objection and bills past it.
3. **CHANGES APPLY NEXT VOYAGE.** — a plausible administrative delay makes the default-donation policy worse.
